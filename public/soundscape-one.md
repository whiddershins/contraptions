## How to use

Open the live app. Play/Pause Music. Walk with arrow keys, WASD, or the mouse. The ball can move on its own (Play/Pause Ball, Angle, Size, Speed, Reset). Volume slider. The live page also links an offices version and the original simpler two-voice version.

## What it is

A 2D field of sound. Voices sit in the plane. You walk; the mix follows. The ball is your position. Position is the instrument.

## Why

A mixer is faders in a list. This is a place. You are in the field. Move, and the sound is already there.

## Privacy

Audio and control stay in the browser. No accounts, no upload.
