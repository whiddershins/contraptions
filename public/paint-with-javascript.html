Surya Narreddi (@kickingkeys) and Cameron Franz RL'd Qwen to paint in p5.js. The model writes a sketch. The sketch is the painting. You edit the code, not the prompt.

They rated 1,664 gens by hand and judged new rollouts pairwise against a love pool. That pool is a folder of gens. Image Compare Workbench is the local set-workbench for that job. Lock A, walk B.
