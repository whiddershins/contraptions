# Paint with JavaScript

Looking at. Surya Narreddi and Cameron Franz RL'd Qwen to paint in p5.js. The sketch is the painting. You edit the code, not the prompt.

They rated 1,664 gens by hand and judged new rollouts pairwise against a love pool. That pool is a folder of gens. Image Compare Workbench is the local set-workbench for that job.
