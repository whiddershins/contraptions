# Soundscape One

A 2D field of sound. You walk; the mix follows. Position is the instrument.

By [Sarth Calhoun](https://sarth.net).

## How to use

Pick a version. Play/Pause Music. Walk with arrow keys, WASD, or the mouse. The ball can move on its own (Play/Pause Ball, Angle, Size, Speed, Reset). Volume is the slider.

## Versions

- [Soundscape One A](https://contraptions.bookofsarth.com/soundscape-one/one-a.html) — four voice. The denser one.
- [Soundscape Two A](https://contraptions.bookofsarth.com/soundscape-one/officeSoundscape_01.html) — offices.
- [Soundscape One B](https://contraptions.bookofsarth.com/soundscape-one/musing_in_two_voices.html) — two voice. The original, simpler one.

## What it is

A playable space, not a playlist. Sound is placed in the plane. Your position is the mix.

## Why

To be inside a sound, not in front of a track.

## Privacy

Audio and control stay in the browser. No accounts, no upload.
